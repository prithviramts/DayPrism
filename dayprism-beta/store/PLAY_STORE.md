# Play Store release checklist

Everything Google asks for, with the answers that match how DayPrism is built.

## 1. Decide before the first upload (can't change later)

- **Package name**: `app.dayprism` (in `app.json` → `android.package`). Once an app is published under a package name, that name is permanent. Change it now if you want something else, for example `com.<yourname>.dayprism`.
- **Signing**: use **Play App Signing** (default). You upload with an *upload key*; Google holds the real signing key. If you build with EAS, it creates and stores the upload key for you.

## 2. Build the release bundle (.aab)

Option A, cloud build with EAS (no local toolchain needed, recommended):

```bash
npx eas-cli@latest login
npx eas-cli@latest build --platform android --profile production
```

Option B, local build: create an upload keystore, add it to `android/app/build.gradle` signing config (see the README), then run `./gradlew bundleRelease` in `dayprism-beta/android`. Note that `android/` is regenerated by `npx expo prebuild`, so EAS is simpler.

Before every new release, bump `version` (shown to users) and `android.versionCode` (must increase) in `app.json`. The `production` EAS profile auto-increments `versionCode`.

## 3. Store listing

- **App name**: DayPrism
- **Short description** (80 chars): `Track sleep, food, caffeine and mood. See what makes your best days. Private.`
- **Full description**:

  > DayPrism helps you understand what makes your days good, or hard.
  >
  > Log your sleep, meals, caffeine, water, activities and quick energy check-ins in a few taps. At the end of the day, write a short reflection and rate your day.
  >
  > Insights connect the dots in your own data: how your sleep affects your energy, whether late caffeine costs you sleep, what skipping breakfast does to your mornings, and where your time actually goes each week. A monthly Review shows streaks, your best and hardest days, and lets you export a summary to discuss with an AI assistant or a coach.
  >
  > Private by design:
  > • Your entries are visible only to you, encrypted on your phone and in transit
  > • Works offline and syncs safely to your account
  > • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data
  > • Optional fingerprint / face / PIN lock
  > • Export your data or delete your account any time

- **App icon**: `store/play-icon-512.png` (512×512)
- **Feature graphic**: `store/feature-graphic-1024x500.png`
- **Phone screenshots**: at least 2 (1080×1920 or larger, 9:16). Take them from a release build with demo data (Welcome → "Explore with demo data"): Today, Insights, History, Review, an entry sheet.
- **Category**: Health & Fitness. **Tags**: habit tracker, sleep tracker, journal.

### Data safety

Entries sync to your Supabase project, so DayPrism **collects** data in Google's sense (it leaves the
device), but does **not share** it (service providers acting for you don't count as sharing).

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Optional | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info → Name | Yes | No | Yes (can be blank) | Account management, App functionality |
| Personal info → Email address | Yes | No | No | Account management |
| Health and fitness → Health info (sleep, mood, energy, meals, caffeine) | Yes | No | No | App functionality |
| App activity → Other user-generated content (reflections, notes) | Yes | No | Yes | App functionality |

- Is all user data encrypted in transit? → **Yes** (HTTPS/TLS)
- Do you provide a way for users to request deletion? → **Yes**: Settings → Delete account (in-app), and
  the web URL where you publish a deletion request page (Google asks for one; a page explaining
  "Settings → Delete account" plus your support email is enough).
- Data is not used for advertising or analytics, and is not sold.

Also complete **App content → Health apps**: DayPrism is a wellness / habit journal, not a medical device.

## 5. Testing track

New personal developer accounts must run a **closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days** before production access. Upload the first .aab to *Testing → Closed testing*, invite testers by email list, then apply for production.

## 6. Before each release

- `npm run typecheck && npm run lint`
- Install the release build on a real phone: first launch, add entries, lock/unlock, export, delete all data
- Update `PRIVACY.md` and `src/app/privacy.tsx` if anything about data handling changed
