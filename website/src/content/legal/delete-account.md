---
title: Delete your DayPrism account
description: How to permanently delete your DayPrism account and all of its data.
updated: 2 October 2026
---
You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, straight from the app. You don't need to contact us or give a reason.

## Delete from the app

1. Open **DayPrism** and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm.

## What gets deleted

- Your account: email address, name and sign-in details.
- Every entry: sleep, meals, caffeine, water, energy and mood check-ins, activities, reflections, custom trackers, cycle logs and your settings.

Deletion from our database is immediate and permanent. Encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten within 30 days. Nothing is kept for advertising, because DayPrism has no ads.

## Want a copy first?

Before deleting, you can export everything from **Settings → Export all data**. The file is created on your phone and you choose where it goes.

## Can't open the app?

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and ask for your account to be deleted. We'll confirm once it's done.
