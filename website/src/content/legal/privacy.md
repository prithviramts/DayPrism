## Summary

DayPrism helps you track your days. Your entries belong to you: only you can read them, we never sell them, and there are no ads or third-party tracking in the app.

## What we store

Your account: email address, name, and a securely hashed password (or your Google account email and name if you sign in with Google). Your entries: sleep, meals, caffeine, water, energy and mood check-ins, activities, reflections and your settings. If you switch on cycle tracking, also your period and symptom logs. Some of this is health and wellness information, and we treat all of it as sensitive.

## Where it is stored

Your entries are kept in an encrypted database on your phone (AES-256), and backed up to your account on our servers, hosted by Supabase. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

## Who can see it

Only you. Access to entries is enforced by the database itself (row-level security). DayPrism administrators can see basic account details (email, name, sign-up date, last active date) to provide support and prevent abuse, but they cannot read your entries.

## Service providers

Supabase (database and sign-in), Google (only if you choose "Continue with Google"), and our email provider (verification and password-reset emails). They process data only to provide these services.

## Health Connect

If you connect Health Connect, DayPrism reads only the data types you allow: steps, exercise sessions and sleep sessions. It uses them only to show your own days and insights, stores them like your other entries, never writes to Health Connect, and never shares or sells this data or uses it for advertising. DayPrism's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can stop importing in Settings and revoke access in Health Connect at any time.

## What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your data, show ads, use advertising identifiers, or include analytics or tracking SDKs.

## Exports

When you export, DayPrism creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet; you choose where it goes. Exported files are not encrypted.

## Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all of your entries from our database straight away. Encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten within 30 days. Signing out removes the copy on your phone.

## Your rights

You can access and export your data (Settings → Export all data), correct it in the app, and delete it (Settings → Delete account). For any other request, contact us.

## Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Optional app lock protects the app with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN. No system is perfectly secure, but we follow industry practice and will notify you if a breach affects your data.

## Not medical advice

DayPrism is a personal wellness journal. It is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition.

## Children

DayPrism is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date below and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

## Contact
